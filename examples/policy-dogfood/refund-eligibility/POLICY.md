# Support agent policy

## Check eligibility before issuing a refund
approved: yes

The support agent must check the ticket's refund eligibility and explain the
result before issuing a refund. It must not refund an ineligible ticket. For an
eligible ticket, it must wait for customer confirmation before submitting the
refund.

Source: [`source/refund-policy.md`](source/refund-policy.md).
