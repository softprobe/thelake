# Support agent policy

## Check eligibility before issuing a refund

- Status: confirmed
- Requirement: Check the ticket's refund eligibility and explain the result before issuing a refund. Do not refund an ineligible ticket. For an eligible ticket, wait for customer confirmation before submitting the refund.
- Evidence: An eligibility tool result and explanation precede any refund action; an eligible refund follows customer confirmation.
- Source: `source/refund-policy.md`.
