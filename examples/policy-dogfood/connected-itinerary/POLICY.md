# Travel agent policy

## Disclose a same-day onward connection before cancellation

- Status: confirmed
- Requirement: Before cancelling a flight leg, inspect the full itinerary. If another same-day segment is connected, name the affected segment and explain the stranding risk before asking the traveler to confirm.
- Evidence: The itinerary lookup result, the assistant disclosure, the traveler confirmation, and the cancellation action appear in that order.
- Source: `source/travel-policy.md` (Flight cancellation handling section).
